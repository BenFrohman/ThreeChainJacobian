# Affine Jacobian length is not \(h^{2,2}\)

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman  
License: Apache-2.0

$$
\dim_{\mathbb{C}} R(F)=\mu(F)=25^3=15625
$$

is the length of the Jacobian ring of the isolated singularity of \(F\) at the origin in \(\mathbb{C}^6\). Steenbrink / \(T_F\) act on that mixed Hodge structure (see SingularityLab).

Primitive \((2,2)\) of the projective fourfold is one graded piece of the same ring:

$$
H^{2,2}_{\mathrm{prim}}(V(F))\simeq R_{12},\qquad 3d-n-2=12.
$$

See `HODGE/docs/GRIFFITHS_JACOBIAN.md`. Neither side inhabits Term B.
